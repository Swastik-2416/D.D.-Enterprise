About the Company

Built with Precision.
Delivered on Time.

Established in 2010, D.D. Enterprise is a dedicated manufacturer of premium precast concrete products in West Bengal. We supply high-grade interlocking paver blocks, chequered tiles, and roof tiles to builders, architects, and government project teams across the region.

Our production facility operates 8 dedicated lines, allowing us to fulfil large-volume orders with consistent quality. Every batch is manufactured to M30–M40 grade concrete specifications with colour-fast pigments that withstand years of outdoor exposure.
